# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2, 2026**

PhotonGainMapViewer is designed with your privacy in mind. This document explains what the app does — and, more importantly, what it doesn't do — with your data.

## No Data Collection

PhotonGainMapViewer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage information. The app does not use analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.

## No Image Analysis or Upload

All images you open are processed entirely on your device. PhotonGainMapViewer does not analyze, scan, or upload your images or their metadata to any server. Your photos never leave your device through this app.

## No Third Parties

Because no data is collected, there is no data to share with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party.

## Network Access

PhotonGainMapViewer does not require or make network connections to function. It works entirely offline.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted here with a revised date at the top.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Please open an issue in this repository (../../issues/new).
